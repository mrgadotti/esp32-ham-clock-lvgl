ESP32-HAM-CLOCK-LVGL

Flash the firmware straight from your browser. Connect the board over USB, click the button for your board and pick the serial port.

Chrome or Edge is required (Web Serial). Firefox and Safari are not supported.

CYD ESP32-2432S028R

ESP32 38-pin + external ILI9341

First run

  1. Tick Erase device the first time this firmware goes onto a board. It clears NVS and leftovers from other projects. Skip it on later updates to keep calibration and WiFi settings.
  2. After flashing, the clock boots and asks for a touch calibration.
  3. With no WiFi configured it starts the access point ESP32-HAM-CLOCK (default password 1234567890). Join it and open http://192.168.4.1 to set WiFi, locator and callsign.
